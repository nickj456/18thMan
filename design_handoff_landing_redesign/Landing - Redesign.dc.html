What's a good drill to improve line speed in defence for an U18 side?
Defensive Line Speed Drill — "Blitz & Shuffle"
Setup: 6 attackers in a flat line, 6 defenders 5m back. Cones mark the D-line start position.
Key coaching points: Push off the back foot, shoulders square, communicate early.
Progression: Add a second phase — defenders reset and go again inside 4 seconds.
