How to share your footage

YouTube (recommended)

  1. Upload your video to YouTube
  2. Set visibility to Unlisted
  3. Copy the link and paste it in the form

Unlisted means only people with the link can view it — it won't appear in search results.

Google Drive

  1. Upload the video to Google Drive
  2. Right-click → Share
  3. Change access to Anyone with the link
  4. Copy and paste the link in the form
